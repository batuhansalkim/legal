Frequently asked

How do I cancel my Premium subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not inside the app. On iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → SplitEasy → Cancel Subscription.

How do I cancel my Premium subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not inside the app. On iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → SplitEasy → Cancel Subscription.

I cancelled but I'm still being charged / I want a refund.
All payments go through the App Store, so refunds are issued by Apple, not by us. Request a refund directly at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I upgraded my phone / reinstalled the app — where's my data?
SplitEasy stores your groups and expenses locally on your device, so this data doesn't automatically transfer between devices or survive a reinstall. Your Premium subscription itself is tied to your Apple ID and can be restored with the "Restore Purchases" button on the Premium screen.

Is my data safe / does SplitEasy see my expenses?
Your groups, expenses, and balances stay on your device — we don't have a server that stores or can see them. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

The AI Trip Planner isn't giving good suggestions.
Suggestions are AI-generated based on your group's quiz answers — try re-taking the quiz with more specific answers, or treat the result as a starting point rather than a final plan.

I found a bug / have a feature request.
Email us at batuhansalkimm@gmail.com — screenshots and steps to reproduce help a lot.
