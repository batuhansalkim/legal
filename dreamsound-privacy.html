Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

DreamSound is built on a simple principle: your sleep is private. We do not sell your data, show you ads, or build profiles about you.

1. Information We Collect

DreamSound is designed to minimize data collection. Here is exactly what we collect and why:

1a. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No name, email address, or account information is required to use the app.
  • We do not create or store user profiles tied to your identity.
  • We do not collect your location data.
  • We do not serve advertisements or share data with ad networks.
  • We do not sell your personal information to any third party.

1b. Anonymous Usage Analytics

We collect limited, anonymous analytics to understand how the app is used and improve it. This data includes:

  • Which sounds are played most frequently (no user identity attached)
  • Screens viewed and paywall/purchase funnel events (e.g., plan selected, purchase completed)
  • Feature usage statistics (e.g., timer usage, mixer usage)
  • Device type and operating system version (non-identifiable)

This data is tied only to a random, anonymous identifier generated on your device — never to your name, email or Apple ID — and is not used for advertising or tracking. Analytics are processed by PostHog (servers in the United States).

2. Analytics Provider (PostHog)

DreamSound uses PostHog to collect the anonymous usage events described in section 1b. Regarding this data:

  • Events are linked to a random identifier created on your device, not to your identity.
  • We do not use analytics data for advertising, and we do not share it with data brokers.
  • PostHog's privacy policy is available at posthog.com/privacy.

3. Subscription & Purchase Data (RevenueCat)

DreamSound uses RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions. When you purchase a subscription:

  • Your payment is processed entirely by Apple (App Store) — we never receive your payment card information.
  • RevenueCat receives an anonymized purchase receipt to verify your subscription status. This receipt does not contain your name or payment details.
  • A random, device-specific identifier is used to link your subscription status to the app — not your Apple ID or personal identity.
  • RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

4. Local Storage

DreamSound stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Your sound preferences and mixer configurations
  • Downloaded audio files (Premium feature)
  • Sleep timer and notification settings
  • Language preference

This data is stored using standard iOS secure storage mechanisms (AsyncStorage) and is not transmitted to our servers.

5. Notifications

If you enable bedtime reminders or timer completion notifications, DreamSound will request notification permission from iOS. Notification scheduling is handled entirely on-device. We do not use push notification services that would require sending your device token to a server.

6. Children's Privacy

DreamSound is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at batuhansalkimm@gmail.com and we will promptly delete such information.

7. Your Rights Under GDPR (European Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights regarding any personal data we process:

  • Right of access: You may request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: You may request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure: You may request deletion of your data at any time.
  • Right to object: You may object to our processing of your data.
  • Right to data portability: You may request your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at batuhansalkimm@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Our legal basis for processing anonymous analytics data is our legitimate interest in improving the application. You may opt out of analytics at any time by contacting us.

8. Your Rights Under CCPA (California Residents)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act:

  • Right to know: You have the right to know what personal information we collect, use, disclose, and sell.
  • Right to delete: You have the right to request deletion of personal information we have collected.
  • Right to opt-out: You have the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information.
  • Right to non-discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your CCPA rights.

To exercise your rights, contact us at batuhansalkimm@gmail.com.

9. Data Deletion

To delete all data associated with your use of DreamSound:

  • Local app data: Delete the app from your device. All locally stored preferences, statistics and downloads are removed with it.
  • Analytics data: Email us at batuhansalkimm@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request." We will remove any analytics data tied to your device identifier within 30 days.
  • Subscription records: These are retained by Apple as required for financial record-keeping. Contact Apple Support to manage your Apple ID purchase history.

10. Third-Party Services Summary

  • RevenueCat — Subscription management. Anonymous purchase verification only.
  • PostHog — Anonymous usage analytics. Not used for advertising or tracking.
  • Apple App Store — Payment processing. Governed by Apple's privacy policy.

We do not integrate with Facebook, Google Analytics, or any advertising SDK.

11. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect any data we process. Because DreamSound collects minimal data and stores most information locally on your device, your exposure is inherently limited. All network communications use industry-standard TLS encryption.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you through the app. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

13. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • Email: batuhansalkimm@gmail.com
  • Response time: We aim to respond within 5 business days.
