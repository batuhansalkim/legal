Overview

The sounds in DreamSound come from three categories:

  • Original recordings — Professionally recorded exclusively for DreamSound.
  • Licensed providers — Sourced from professional audio libraries under commercial licenses.
  • Creative Commons — Community-contributed sounds from Freesound.org under open licenses, with full attribution below.

Audio Processing

All sounds in DreamSound — regardless of source — have been professionally processed for use in a sleep and focus application:

  • Seamlessly looped to eliminate clicks and pops at loop boundaries
  • Normalized and mastered to consistent loudness levels (−14 LUFS)
  • Encoded as high-quality AAC audio at 256 kbps
  • Optimized for gentle fade-in and fade-out transitions
