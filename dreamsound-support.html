Manage or cancel your subscription

  • Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  • Select DreamSound and tap Cancel Subscription.
  • To avoid being charged, cancel at least 24 hours before your free trial or current period ends.
  • Your Premium access stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.

Restore purchases

Bought Premium on another device or reinstalled the app? Open DreamSound, go to the Premium screen and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

Refunds

All payments are processed by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Frequently asked questions

  • Does audio keep playing when my screen is locked? Yes. DreamSound plays in the background, and the sleep timer fades the sound out for you.
  • Can I listen offline? Premium members can download sounds and play them without an internet connection.
  • Do I need an account? No. DreamSound works without sign-up or login.
  • How do I delete my data? Deleting the app removes all data stored on your device. See our Privacy Policy for analytics data deletion.
